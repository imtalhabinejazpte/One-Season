How much does a repair cost?

Your technician diagnoses the problem first and gives you the exact price before any work starts. Season Pass members pay a lower diagnostic fee: $95, $59 or $49 depending on the plan.

What does a new system cost?

It depends on the size of your home and the system you choose. We share what our Orange County customers actually paid in our cost breakdown, and we quote your home in person with no pressure.

Do you service all brands?

Yes. Our licensed technicians service and maintain all major makes and models of heating and air conditioning equipment.

What warranty comes with a new install?

New installations come with a 10-year parts and 2-year labor warranty, plus a free check-in visit after six months.

What is the Season Pass?

Our maintenance plan. You get spring and fall tune-ups, faster arrival windows and member pricing on repairs. Every month of membership also earns Rainy Day Fund credit toward a future system.

Is there a contract? Can I cancel?

No long-term contracts. Upgrade, downgrade or cancel anytime, with no penalties.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. See our financing options for new systems.
